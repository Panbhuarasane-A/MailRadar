# Privacy Policy for Mail Hinge AI

**Last Updated**: September 20, 2026

Mail Hinge AI ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy and securing your personal communications. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning data collection, usage, and Google API compliance.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

- **Account Profile Information**: Name, email address, and authentication identifiers obtained through Google OAuth.
- **Email Content via Gmail API (`gmail.readonly`)**: Message headers, subject lines, senders, body snippets, and full message text to calculate priority rankings and extract actionable tasks.
- **Telegram Channel Data**: Public job announcements and application links ingested from authorized Telegram channels.

---

## 2. Purpose and Use of Data

We process your information solely to provide email organization and productivity features:
1. **Urgency & Priority Scoring**: Automatically scoring emails (0–100) to highlight critical messages.
2. **Action Item & Deadline Extraction**: Generating Kanban task boards and daily briefing summaries.
3. **ATS Link Bypass**: Resolving intermediate shortlinks directly to official employer career portals.

---

## 3. Strict Prohibition on Data Commercialization

- **Zero Advertising**: We never use or transfer your email data for serving advertisements, retargeting, or market research.
- **No Public AI Model Training**: Your private emails are **NEVER** used to train public foundation models.
- **No Data Brokering**: We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties.

---

## 4. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Mail Hinge AI's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the **Limited Use** requirements.

---

## 5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You may disconnect your Gmail mailbox or delete your Mail Hinge AI profile at any time in the application settings. Upon deletion, your OAuth tokens and synced data are permanently purged from our database.
